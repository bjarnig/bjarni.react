Crossings: WsHear

WsMap with an input, here its own output fed back through a filter or a delay, one 64-sample block late as through LocalIn. Each heard waveset is read back as a state on the same lo..hi scale, and couple mixes it into x before the map steps. A filter keeps the period of a periodic signal, so a stable fixed point is left alone; a period 2 orbit can collapse onto one length, turn chaotic, or be tamed.

Start with the gain low.
