VOSIM: Several in parallel

Tempelaars: combine several VOSIM functions with the same period and different pulse widths, and the signal has as many formant peaks. Three of them share one pitch here, each with its own F and level. The vowel buttons set typical first, second and third formants (approximate male averages after Peterson and Barney, 1952); they are illustrations, not values from the VOSIM literature.
